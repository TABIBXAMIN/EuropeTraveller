ABOUT EUROPE TRAVELLER

About
Europe Traveller.

Europe Traveller helps you find places to visit in Europe. Read about countries, get travel tips and plan your trip.

WHY EUROPE TRAVELLER?

Good places.
Clear advice.

Finding a place to visit should be exciting. It can also be hard to know where to start. Europe Traveller brings together country information, travel photos and useful tips so you can explore your choices in one place.

This website is for people who enjoy discovering new places. You might be travelling for the first time or looking for a different kind of holiday. Use the country guides to get ideas. Then read the travel tips and choose the things that matter most to you.

You do not need to visit every famous place to enjoy a trip. A walk through a town, a meal in a local cafe or time beside a lake can be part of the holiday too. The aim is to help you find places you would like to visit and make a plan that works for you.

HOW TO USE THIS WEBSITE

Plan your trip in three steps.

01

Choose a country

Look at the destinations. Choose a country and read about places to visit. Compare city trips, seaside stays and time in the countryside before you decide.

02

Get ready to go

Read tips on packing, planning your spending and travelling between places. Think about the length of your trip and the things you will need each day.

03

Make your plan

Choose your dates and write down the things you want to do. Keep the list realistic so you have time to enjoy each place and take breaks.
