MAKE A PLAN THAT WORKS FOR YOU

Start with a few clear choices.

01

Choose what matters most

Write down the things you would most like to see. These could be a famous building, a beach, a museum or a place with a good view. Use this list to decide where to spend your time.

02

Keep nearby places together

Look at a map before deciding what to do each day. Places that are close to each other may fit into the same day. This can reduce travel time and help you spend more time exploring.

03

Leave some free time

You do not need to fill every hour. Leave time for meals, rest and places you find during the trip. A plan can help you get started while still giving you room to change your mind.
